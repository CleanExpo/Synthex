# Synthex Rules v1

Source basis: Synthex repo README, marketing master agent notes, brand-config source-of-truth docs, and marketing asset framework. This is a conservative publishing/approval policy: strong enough for autonomous drafting, not a license to publish without the gates below.

## Brand Voice (non-negotiable)

- Tone:
  - Confident, operator-grade, practical, evidence-led.
  - Smart but not smug; ambitious but not hype-driven.
  - Position Synthex as an AI-powered social media agency/platform, not a magic button.

- Do language:
  - “AI-powered content intelligence”
  - “brand voice learning”
  - “platform-specific optimization”
  - “approval workflows”
  - “performance insights”
  - “content calendar”
  - “multi-platform publishing”
  - “helps teams create, schedule, analyze, and improve content”

- Don’t language:
  - “guaranteed viral”
  - “dominate every platform” unless framed as aspiration/positioning, not promise
  - “set-and-forget growth”
  - “replace your entire marketing team”
  - “guaranteed ROI”
  - “secret algorithm hack”
  - “fully compliant automatically”

- Reading level:
  - Grade 7–9 for public marketing.
  - Grade 10–12 acceptable for technical/operator docs.
  - Prefer short sentences. Avoid jargon unless immediately explained.

- Formatting style:
  - Clear headings, bullets, and short paragraphs.
  - Lead with the practical outcome, then explain the mechanism.
  - Use examples over abstract claims.
  - For long-form: include TL;DR, sections, cited sources, practical checklist, and final CTA.

## Offer Rules

- Core offers:
  - AI-powered social media agency/platform in one workspace.
  - Brand voice learning and content generation.
  - Multi-platform content planning, optimization, and publishing workflows.
  - Analytics and performance reporting.
  - Competitor/content intelligence.
  - Team/client collaboration and approval workflows.
  - White-label or agency-friendly client management, where product support exists.

- Disallowed claims:
  - Guaranteed followers, revenue, conversions, virality, rankings, or ROI.
  - Claims that Synthex can publish to or access a platform unless that integration is verified for the account/environment.
  - Claims that AI output is always accurate, original, compliant, or safe without review.
  - Claims that Synthex provides legal, financial, medical, tax, employment, compliance, or regulated-industry advice.
  - Competitor attack claims that are not cited, current, and objective.

- Mandatory disclaimers:
  - “Results vary by market, offer, audience, content quality, budget, and execution.”
  - “AI-generated content should be reviewed before publishing.”
  - “Platform availability and features may vary by account, API access, and plan.”
  - For strategy content: “This is general marketing information, not legal, financial, or professional advice.”

- CTA patterns allowed:
  - “Book a demo”
  - “Join the waitlist”
  - “Start building your content engine”
  - “See how Synthex would plan your next 30 days of content”
  - “Turn one idea into a full campaign”
  - “Audit your current social workflow”
  - “Request access”

## Audience Rules

- Primary ICP:
  - Small to mid-market businesses, founders, and lean marketing teams that need a social media operating system without hiring a full agency.
  - Marketing agencies managing multiple client brands and approval workflows.

- Secondary ICP:
  - Creators, consultants, coaches, and entrepreneurs who need consistent, on-brand multi-platform content.
  - Franchise/multi-location operators needing repeatable brand-safe content workflows.

- Pain points to prioritize:
  - Inconsistent posting and weak content cadence.
  - Running out of ideas.
  - Content that does not match brand voice.
  - Too many platforms and too little time.
  - Manual scheduling, reporting, and repurposing.
  - Difficulty proving social media contribution to pipeline or customer engagement.
  - Agency/client approval friction.

- Topics to avoid:
  - Platform manipulation, fake engagement, botting, spam, scraping that violates platform terms, or black-hat growth tactics.
  - Political persuasion, targeted political profiling, or inflammatory culture-war content.
  - Regulated advice: legal, medical, financial, investment, tax, employment, insurance advice.
  - Unsupported competitor takedowns.
  - Security/privacy claims beyond verified product controls.

## E-E-A-T Rules

- Minimum citation count per long-form piece:
  - Blog/article under 1,000 words: minimum 3 credible citations.
  - Blog/article 1,000–2,000 words: minimum 5 credible citations.
  - Guide/whitepaper over 2,000 words: minimum 8 credible citations.
  - Product pages may use fewer citations, but every factual market/platform/compliance claim must still be verifiable.

- Allowed source tiers:
  - Tier 1: Official platform documentation and APIs: LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit, Threads, etc.
  - Tier 1: Government/regulator/security standards where relevant: FTC, ACCC, OAIC, NIST, CISA, ICO, ASA/CAP, platform policy docs.
  - Tier 2: Primary research, peer-reviewed papers, reputable benchmark reports, first-party survey data with methodology.
  - Tier 2: Established marketing/advertising publications where claims are attributed and not overgeneralized.
  - Tier 3: Expert commentary, podcasts, newsletters, and blogs only when used as perspective, not as proof.

- Forbidden source types:
  - Unsourced AI-generated articles.
  - Anonymous SEO farms, content mills, scraped summaries, or pages with no author/editorial accountability.
  - Forum posts or social posts as factual proof unless they are the object being analyzed.
  - Outdated platform growth statistics without date/context.
  - Competitor marketing pages used as objective proof without corroboration.

- Claim verification requirement:
  - Every metric, trend, compliance statement, platform rule, and competitor comparison must have a citation or be removed.
  - If a claim cannot be verified in under 10 minutes, mark it NEEDS_REVIEW rather than publishing.
  - Keep citations near the claim they support.
  - Never cite a source that does not actually support the sentence.

## Channel Rules

### LinkedIn

- Post length:
  - Standard: 900–1,500 characters.
  - Max: 3,000 characters.
  - Use 1–3 short sections or bullets for readability.

- Hook style:
  - Operator insight, contrarian but grounded observation, short problem statement, or “what changed / what to do now.”
  - Avoid bait hooks, fake vulnerability, and exaggerated income claims.

- CTA style:
  - Soft professional CTA.
  - Examples: “If you want the checklist, comment ‘workflow’.” “Want us to audit this process? Book a demo.” “Save this for your next content planning session.”

### X/Twitter

- Max length:
  - Single post: 280 characters.
  - Prefer 180–240 characters when no media/link is included.

- Thread rules:
  - 4–8 posts for tactical breakdowns.
  - First post must state the problem or promise clearly without hype.
  - Each post should contain one idea.
  - Last post must include a concise CTA or practical next step.
  - No engagement bait, fake scarcity, or unverified metrics.

### Blog

- Word count:
  - Standard SEO/operator post: 900–1,500 words.
  - Deep guide: 1,800–2,500 words.
  - Product update: 500–900 words.

- Required sections:
  - TL;DR
  - Who this is for
  - Problem / context
  - Practical framework or workflow
  - Where Synthex fits, if relevant
  - Risks / limitations / what still needs human review
  - Sources / further reading
  - CTA

## Compliance / Safety

- No guaranteed outcomes claims.
- No legal, medical, financial, tax, employment, or regulated professional advice language.
- No competitor defamation.
- No fake scarcity or fabricated testimonials.
- No unverifiable “best”, “only”, “#1”, “most advanced”, or “guaranteed” claims unless formally evidenced and approved.
- No platform-policy circumvention guidance.
- Escalate if uncertain.

## Approval Gates

- Auto-approve if:
  - The asset is an internal draft, idea list, or non-published planning note.
  - No performance guarantee, regulated advice, competitor claim, platform-rule claim, or customer data is included.
  - Brand fit >= 8, evidence >= 7, conversion >= 7, and total score >= 24/30.
  - Long-form citations meet the minimum count and support the claims.

- Must review if:
  - Any claim references platform policy, API capability, pricing, integration support, compliance, security, privacy, ROI, benchmark data, or competitor comparison.
  - Content mentions customer/user outcomes, case studies, screenshots, analytics, or testimonials.
  - Content is scheduled for public publishing, paid ads, sales collateral, investor material, or onboarding flows.
  - Any score is below threshold but not a hard block.

- Must block if:
  - Contains guaranteed outcomes, regulated advice, fake testimonials, fabricated statistics, defamatory competitor claims, or platform manipulation tactics.
  - Uses private customer data or screenshots without approval.
  - Cites unsupported or forbidden sources for factual claims.
  - Claims a live integration, automation, publishing capability, or compliance posture that is not verified in the target environment.
  - Brand fit < 6, evidence < 6 for factual content, conversion < 5 for sales content, or total score < 21/30.

## Scoring Rubric

- Brand fit score /10:
  - 9–10: Confident, useful, specific, brand-safe, clearly Synthex.
  - 7–8: Usable with light edits.
  - 5–6: Generic, too hypey, too vague, or off-tone; review required.
  - 0–4: Unsafe, misleading, or not Synthex.

- Evidence score /10:
  - 9–10: Claims are cited, current, and directly supported.
  - 7–8: Most claims supported; minor citation gaps only.
  - 5–6: Useful but under-cited; review required.
  - 0–4: Unsupported, stale, or misleading claims; block.

- Conversion score /10:
  - 9–10: Clear ICP, clear pain, strong but honest CTA, practical next step.
  - 7–8: Good commercial direction; could be sharper.
  - 5–6: Weak CTA or unclear audience; review required.
  - 0–4: No clear buyer value or uses unsafe persuasion; block.

- Publish threshold:
  - Auto-approve draft-to-queue only: 24/30 minimum, no individual score below 7, and no must-review/must-block trigger.
  - Human review required for public publishing unless a separate approved autopublish policy exists for the channel and asset type.
  - Block below 21/30 or on any must-block trigger.

## Why every hook is four to six words

`taste/ra.md` records it as a measured constraint, not a stylistic preference.
The type ramp is re-anchored to canvas width (hook ≥ 8.15% of width), so at
1200 wide the hook is 98px and a seven-word hook costs three lines and half the
board. §5 permits seven words; RA's landscape assets cannot afford them.

## Voice check

**`voice.forbiddenWords`** — `FORBIDDEN_PRONOUNS` (we / our / i / us / my) plus
RA's own list. None appears. Note that every hook is an imperative addressed to
the reader's own action, which is how the pronoun ban is written around rather
than fought: "Draw the line", "Mark the wall", "Finish the job".

**`doNot`** — checked line by line:

- No abbreviation to "RA" anywhere; the eyebrow always reads `RestoreAssist`.
- No red as a brand colour; the palette is navy / warm earth / tan only.
- **No urgency.** No deadline, no countdown, no "before it's too late". `ra.ts`
  is explicit that the tradie reading this already has urgency.
- Active voice throughout. Every hook is an imperative or a completed action.
- No technical term without plain English. "Inspection report", "readings" and
  "record" are the trade's own words, not jargon needing a gloss.
- The brand is never positioned before the reader's problem: the eyebrow is a
  caption-type identifier, and the first _statement_ on every board is about the
  reader's work.
- No CTA driving to a brand destination. "Start free" directs the reader to act
  in their own interest; the domain is deliberately not used as a CTA.
- No competitor named — DocuSketch, Encircle, Magicplan and Xactimate appear
  nowhere.
- No standalone "AI-powered" — the word AI does not appear at all.

**Standing prohibitions from `ra.claims.md`** — no price in any form, no
aggregate rating or review count, no app-store claim, no NCC 2022 in any form,
and no S500 reference at all. The "S500:2021-aligned" wording is a _candidate_
awaiting sign-off rather than an approved fact, so it was designed around rather
than placeholdered.

**Reading level** — `pillars.readingLevel` targets 4 (hard fail 8). Every line
is a short simple sentence in Australian English; the longest is nine words.

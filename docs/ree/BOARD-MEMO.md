DECISION

**REVISE BEFORE PHASE 2.** Retain the shared loop and truth-first policy. Reject current sequencing and implied roster size. Require all seven specification amendments below and founder approval before Phase 2 implementation. This is strategic review, not a release PASS, purchase approval or evidence of deployed success.

RATIONALE

The strongest choice is refusing to present failed reads as live measurements. Phase 2 nevertheless repairs too few paths: L4-synthex-audit.md gaps 5/8/10/11 identify persistence, tenant, probe and ingestion weaknesses beyond the named stubs. Reliable dashboards require reliable evidence chains.

Earlier observation reduces uncertainty; premature infrastructure amplifies it. Preserve Vercel Workflow as first candidate, with Inngest as comparator, while making safe execution and full cost decisive (L5-durable-agents.md §2, Durable execution). No vendor selection is authorised here.

TOP 7 REQUIRED CHANGES TO THE SPEC

1. **Define winning and scope — §§1, 3, 11, 15.** Add per-brand customer/intent, legitimate geography, accountable owner and conversion contract: qualified enquiries, booked outcomes and contribution where observable. Separate visibility from commercial outcomes. Restore CleanExpo to rollout; explain CCW's addition instead of silently substituting it. Inventory all five brands and client businesses; select DR plus one founder-approved contrasting pilot after eligibility/access checks. Set baseline, useful effect, cost ceiling and expansion gate. Evidence: L2-local-eeat.md F2/F15; L3-media-ads-brand.md M1–M4.

2. **Make evidence honest end-to-end — §§5, 6, 8, 12, 14.** Define zero, unavailable, unauthorised, unsupported, stale and partial states. Record numerator/denominator, completeness, source/fetch time, source timezone, tenant, surface, interface/model and configuration version. Map O/A/I/L to evidence tags without converting announcements into account verification. Require tenant OAuth, pagination/reconciliation and selected retired-client repair or disabling. Label model probes and heuristic GEO/E-E-A-T scores as diagnostics. Separate mentions, citations and outcomes; never add overlapping GSC subsets. Spoofed bot user-agents test synthetic access, not verified crawler access. Evidence: L1-platforms.md G4/G6; L4-synthex-audit.md gaps 5/8/10/11/15.

3. **Move experimental design earlier — §§6, 8 A09–A10, 11.** Establish frozen panels/grids and baselines during revised Phase 2, using permitted exports if necessary. Record controls, prior trends, seasonality, hours, campaigns and concurrent edits. Remove “until the update calendar says otherwise”: silence cannot clear confounding. Three observations are not a sufficiency rule; estimate variance, useful effect and observation window. Include matched non-winners. Pre-register keep/inconclusive/stop criteria and require replication before promoting causal claims. Evidence: L2-local-eeat.md F15; L5-durable-agents.md §2, What AI-visibility measurements actually mean.

4. **Shrink the roster and reorder phases — §§2, 9, 11.** Start Collector, Analyst/Experiment Steward and Evidence Editor sharing typed adapters; Sentinel, crawl and entity checks become capabilities. Add a deterministic executor at the write gate. Sequence honesty/access/baselines → read-only pilot → approved durability spike → gated interventions → justified expansion. Inventory existing real assets early; defer separate media and paid-search agents. Deterministic fetches need no LLM. Evidence: L5-durable-agents.md §4; L3-media-ads-brand.md I1/I3.

5. **Specify approval and evidence boundaries — §§6, 8, 9, 12, 13.** Bind approval to tenant, authorised actor, target account, canonical payload hash, asset IDs, budget, expiry and policy version; changes invalidate approval. Recheck before execution. Gate every reply, invitation, IndexNow submission and external write, closing §9's “sensitive reply” exception. Reconcile uncertain writes before retrying; retain receipts and public/moderation read-back. Enforce tool, destination and tenant restrictions outside model prompts. Define source-specific retention/deletion, consent, private-address handling and real-photo rights; no synthetic-image exception. Expert-review safety/insurance claims. Evidence: L2-local-eeat.md F7/F10/F18; L4-synthex-audit.md gaps 3/7/8; L5-durable-agents.md §2, Evaluation and action safety.

6. **Make engine selection falsifiable — §§10, 12, 15.** Founder approves dependency/cost before the spike. Compare identical workloads on approved candidates: crash-after-write-before-receipt, early/duplicate/revoked/expired approvals, cancellation, revoked credentials, 429s and redeploy/version changes during a real multi-day wait. Demand zero duplicate test-target effects and no lost approvals. Evaluate retention/export, region, ownership and AUD total cost. Map immutable approvals, operation uniqueness and reconciliation to existing models; insufficiency requires schema approval. Do not rebuild the entire legacy queue concurrently. Evidence: L4-synthex-audit.md gaps 1–4/6; L5-durable-agents.md §2/§4.

Add versioned task/trace evals for unsupported claims, conflicting evidence, injection, tenant leakage, stale approvals and recovery. Require zero permission/isolation violations and pre-agreed factual-support thresholds. Make relevant integration tests blocking; verify actual required checks. Fresh repo-defined tests and genuinely independent same-final-SHA review precede push/PR; human merge remains mandatory. Persona debate cannot supply that review. Evidence: L4-synthex-audit.md gap 14; both process-owner definitions.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS DECISION

- No lawful account data or usable outcome baseline: narrow to manual factual audits.
- Neither approved engine meets recovery/cost limits: retain bounded manual operation, without quietly adding another vendor.
- Adequately observed pilots repeatedly show no useful benefit: retire the hypothesis, not inconvenient evidence.

NEXT ACTIONS

First 30 days:

1. **Days 1–7 — Senior PM + founder:** obtain seven amendments and phase approval; select pilots, budgets and metric owners. Done: approved work orders and account/rights matrix resolving the critical access gap; blocked surfaces excluded.
2. **Days 8–21 — Engineering + Analyst:** repair selected honesty/isolation paths, validate conversions, freeze baselines and run read-only packets; conduct only an approved engine spike. Done: evidence, eval results, recovery/cost comparison and explicit remaining gaps.
3. **Days 22–30 — founder + Experiment Steward:** approve at most one change per independent test unit if prerequisites pass. Done: exact approved payload, receipt/read-back and scheduled outcome review, or documented hold. Thirty days promises no ranking result.

RISK TO WATCH

**Riskiest assumption:** competitor/citation differences can become controllable, profitable gains for these businesses. Reliable automation can industrialise a false causal story. Controls, business outcomes and retained inconclusive results are the defence.

KILL-SWITCHES

- Unauthorised write, tenant leak, duplicate effect, fabricated evidence or prohibited tactic: disable affected executor; incident review and founder-approved restart.
- Missing/revoked consent, stale approval, unsupported imagery, disputed retention rights or uncertain external state: quarantine action; no blind retry or automatic Google-edit reversal.
- Approved AUD cap exhausted or unbounded retries/fan-out: stop paid calls/new runs; preserve reconciliation evidence.
- Three consecutive failures of one step, regression or absent release evidence: stop affected lane and escalate.
- Broken controls, excessive missingness or failed pre-registered outcome threshold: suspend learning/promotion and review continuation. Set thresholds before the experiment.

Rollback compensates; it cannot erase exposure, messages or spend. Test global/tenant stops at dispatch and immediately before side effects. Reconcile already uncertain writes.

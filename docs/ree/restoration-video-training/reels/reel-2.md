## The message

- "Here is the real process, not the shortcut version."
- The numbered steps on screen are the whole story. The long caption repeats them in plain words and ends: skipping any one step is how mould comes back months later.
- Message channel: on-screen step labels plus caption. No voice.

## Transcript

No speech. The audio is a licensed music track (lyrics not transcribed or reproduced). The on-screen labels, paraphrased:

- Step 1: contain the work area.
- Step 2: set negative air controls.
- Step 3: remove the affected materials.
- Step 4: clean the work area thoroughly (on-screen wording is crude slang with an angry-face emoji).
- Step 5: run HEPA air filtration devices for 48 hours ("devices" misspelt on screen).
- Step 7: pass the PRV (post-remediation verification), with a relieved-face emoji.
- Step 6 never appears. The count jumps from 5 to 7.

## Production style

- Format: single-take walk-through, numbered steps as small white condensed text at mid-frame.
- Graphics: text only, small (about 2 percent of frame height): hard to read on a phone.
- Audio: trending-style music, no voice. Loud and peaking.
- Look: bright, clean, white walls, teal tape as a strong visual accent: the containment itself is the visual.
- What a producer would copy: a tidy, taped, wrapped job site is the credibility signal. The process list is a ready-made script structure.

## Gaps

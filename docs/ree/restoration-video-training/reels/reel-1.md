## The message

- Thesis (on the card, not in the voice): mould returns when a contractor removes the mould but not the cause.
- Caption expands it: the moisture path, the humidity conditions, and the pressure dynamics that pull contaminated air through walls were never fixed. "Understand your home as a system."
- Message channel: the card carries the argument; the voice carries the evidence (an inspector's site walk).

## Gaps (what we do not have to make this)

- Our own footage of an opened wall with staining in an Australian house (brick veneer, plasterboard, timber frame).
- An inspector PiP recorded on site.
- A lab result or moisture reading to show the "cause" on screen.

Lesson for our scripts: lead with the cause, not the mould. The homeowner's real question is "will it come back?"

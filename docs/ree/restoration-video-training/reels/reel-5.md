## The message

- If you need to seal a wall cavity for negative pressure inside a remediation zone, this tape does it: one span across the studs, cut at each stud, fold in, squeegee down. Also works on supply ducts.
- Caption adds (not shown in the video): checking containment tightness with a smoke pen and manometer, and static pressure on the lay-flat duct for negative air. It is a brand shout-out to Retrotec.
- Message channel: casual voice plus a top text card that teases the brand.

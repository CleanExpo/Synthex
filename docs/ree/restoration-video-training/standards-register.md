# Standards register: restoration video claims

Every procedural or safety statement a restoration video makes must map to a row here. A claim
not in the register, or in it with any status other than VERIFIED, cannot be stated as fact on
screen or in narration.

How a row earns VERIFIED: the lead session (not a helper agent) opened the source on 08/11/2026
and read text that supports the finding. Helper-agent findings stay UNVERIFIED until reopened.
Paywalled standards are NEEDS-PRIMARY-SOURCE until someone with a licensed copy checks the clause.

## The two standards that govern Australian work

Both were confirmed on the Standards Australia store on 08/11/2026:

- **AS-IICRC S500:2025**, Standard for Professional Water Damage Restoration. Published 28/03/2025. Adopts ANSI/IICRC S500:2021 with Australian modifications. https://store.standards.org.au/product/as-iicrc-s500-2025
- **AS-IICRC S520:2025**, Standard for Professional Mould Remediation. Published 28/11/2025. Adopts ANSI/IICRC S520:2024 (4th edition) with Australian modifications in Appendix ZZ. Prepared by committee ME-094, Mould and Water Restoration. https://store.standards.org.au/product/as-iicrc-s520-2025

Australian standards are voluntary unless called up in law or a contract (stated in the S520
preview). In practice insurers and scopes call them up. Neither text is held by Unite Group;
buying both is the single biggest step to moving NEEDS-PRIMARY-SOURCE rows to VERIFIED (a
spending decision for Phill, not taken here).

## Five corrections every Australian video makes against the US reels

1. Respirators are P2 (minimum) or P3, fitted and fit-checked. Never "N95" or "P100".
2. Before any cut in an older home: asbestos (pre-1990), lead paint (pre-1970), live wiring. Show or state the check.
3. No numbers that no standard supports: no "48 hours of scrubbing", no "2 feet past the stain", no pressure figure unless it is from the job's written plan, in pascals.
4. No mould licence implied. Credentials named are real ones (IICRC WRT, AMRT, ASD) and only if the person holds them.
5. No "kills mould" claims. Removal is the method; any product claim needs its Australian registration.

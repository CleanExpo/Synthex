Date: 18 October 2026. Author: research-next-level fleet unit (Fleet-Role: reader). Base: `origin/fleet-unit/p2/base` at `7453ab44`.

P10 billing was retired (AM-7, DECISIONS A-25), so the Stripe and billing references below are historical. "Client" in this note means a Unite-Group business (for example CARSI or RestoreAssist), and the competitor set is a capability benchmark, not a market to win.

Scope. This note adds to the vibe-to-leader spec and its amendments. It does not re-open anything the spec settles: the finish line, the 14-competitor set, the Top-25 enablers, the SWOT, M1 to M14, the JEV harness, P-1 to P9, and amendment AM-3 (P10 billing in Stripe test mode, since retired by AM-7). Evidence tags follow `.claude/rules/fabel-evidence-standard.md`. A URL tagged VERIFIED was opened directly on 18 October 2026, either with WebFetch or with `curl` on the `.md` form of the page. ## Summary

- **Already well covered.** The spec covers containment, measurement, the machine contract, outcomes and delegated publish. No large plan gap was found there.
- **Gap 1: the Australian legal layer.** No gate checks Spam Act unsubscribe rules in messages, or state licence-number rules in trade ads. Both carry six-figure penalties or licence risk today.
- **Gap 2: the ad-platform loop.** Leads carry no click id, so verified calls and bookings can never go back to Google Ads or Meta. Without that, those ad platforms cannot bid on real outcomes.
- **Gap 3: missing local-business tools.** Leaders sell missed-call text-back, call tracking with number swapping, multi-engine AI-answer tracking, AI-crawler analytics and a unified inbox. Synthex has the parts (Twilio, GBP, a geo-citation monitor, mention fetch) but not these capabilities.
- **Gap 4: the fleet uses a small slice of Claude Code.** It registers three command hooks. None of them fails closed, and the Bash deny hook still writes a key Claude Code does not document. Prompt and agent hooks, `TaskCompleted`, `ConfigChange`, skill frontmatter limits and `claude plugin eval` are all unused.

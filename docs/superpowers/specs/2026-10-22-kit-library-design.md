# Pre-built kit library: design spec

**Status:** build-order step 1 (schema, catalogue, manifests, guard test) built on `feature/kit-library-catalogue`; kits K1–K14 themselves not built. **Date:** 22/10/2026.
**Founder decisions folded in (22/10/2026, relayed by the Synthex coordinator):** Synthex creates GA4 properties under authority the client grants at onboarding; at most US$10–12 direct spend per client build; CARSI is not an RTO; CARSI and RestoreAssist sites are hosted on DigitalOcean.
**Client metric:** both. Every kit is ranked by how much time it takes off the next CARSI enrolment or RestoreAssist trial sign-up job.

## The goal in plain English

Today each client job is assembled by hand: an agent works out how to connect Search Console, writes schema from scratch, rebuilds a posting calendar, and hardcodes the client's details in a script. The goal is to build each of those pieces once, test it, store it in Synthex, and let an agent pick it up for the next client.

That needs two things:

1. **Kits.** A kit is one repeatable job, done once properly: its steps, its templates, the code that runs it, the check that proves it worked, and the compliance rules it carries. Example: "search-engine onboarding" verifies a domain with Google and Bing, submits the sitemap, and pings IndexNow on every publish.
2. **A client manifest.** One record per client holding the facts every kit needs: brand slug, domains, Google Business Profile location, connected platforms, approved-claims file, compliance flags (for example CARSI's training-organisation status), onboarding authority, hosting, and the per-build cost ceiling. Every fact carries its source; anything the repo does not hold is `null` and listed in `unknown`. Today this does not exist: `context/clients/` holds only a README, there is one campaign fixture (`lib/marketing-agency/fixtures/restoreassist.ts`), and campaign inputs are hardcoded in `scripts/generate-*-campaign.ts`.

A client job becomes: load the client manifest, pick the kits, run them, check each kit's verifier.

## Where kits live

In the Synthex repo as typed code, not a database table and not Claude Code skills.

- `lib/kits/schema.ts`: zod schemas for `Kit` and `ClientManifest`.
- `lib/kits/catalogue.ts`: the catalogue below as data.
- `lib/kits/clients/carsi.ts`, `lib/kits/clients/restoreassist.ts`: the first two client manifests.
- `tests/unit/kits/catalogue.test.ts`: fails when a kit is missing a required field or names a repo path that does not exist, so the catalogue cannot silently rot.

Why not a table: there is no Kit/Playbook/Recipe model in `prisma/schema.prisma`, and a new one needs a migration under a protected path. Why not skills: skills serve Claude Code sessions, not the running product. The catalogue can move to a table later without changing its shape.

## Kit fields

| Field                | Meaning                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`, `name`         | Stable id (`K1`…), short name                                                                                                               |
| `outcome`            | What the client gets                                                                                                                        |
| `clientMetric`       | `carsi_enrolments`, `ra_trial_signups`, `both`, or `none: internal plumbing`                                                                |
| `inputs`             | Client-manifest fields the kit reads                                                                                                        |
| `artefacts`          | Each one `in-repo` (path, wired, tested), `in-skills` (path, port needed) or `missing` (open-source source with licence, or build in-house) |
| `verifier`           | The check that proves the kit worked (a JEV goal or test)                                                                                   |
| `compliance`         | Australian rules the kit carries                                                                                                            |
| `founderGates`       | Keys, OAuth scopes, protected paths, spend                                                                                                  |
| `minutesSavedPerJob` | Estimate; the ranking key                                                                                                                   |
| `maxDirectCostUsd`   | Estimated direct spend (model calls, paid APIs) per run, marked `estimate` until measured; capped at the per-build ceiling                  |
| `readiness`          | `ready`, `partial`, `missing`                                                                                                               |

## Australian compliance every kit carries

- **Spam Act 2003:** consent, sender identification, a working unsubscribe, opt-out honoured within 5 working days with no login or fee.
- **Australian Consumer Law ss18/29 (ACCC):** no fake, undisclosed-incentive or cherry-picked reviews. Guidance: https://www.accc.gov.au/system/files/online-review-and-testimonials.pdf
- **Privacy Act 1988 / APPs:** APP 7 for direct marketing, APP 8 for cross-border disclosure, privacy policy covering analytics and pixels. Automated-decision disclosure in privacy policies from about 10/12/2026 under the 2024 amendment [UNVERIFIED date].
- **CARSI:** not a registered training organisation; it is a company insured as a training organisation for Australian insurance purposes (founder, 22/10/2026). No claim of national recognition or AQF accreditation, and never describe CARSI as an RTO (`packages/brand-config/src/brands/carsi.claims.md`).
- **RestoreAssist:** trial-to-paid terms shown clearly.

## Build order

Ranked by readiness times time saved on the next CARSI or RestoreAssist job.

1. **Client manifest plus the typed catalogue and its guard test.** Nothing can be "grabbed" without it. Small, no protected paths.
2. **K1 search-engine onboarding.** Most of it exists and is tested; adds Bing Webmaster submission and IndexNow, and retires the old ping URLs (checked: both retired, see K1).
3. **K2 measurement.** GA4 property creation under the onboarding grant, Data API pull and a lead-event tracking plan; unblocks K14 reporting.
4. **K3 schema packs.** Port the 27 skill templates into typed builders per business type.
5. **K5 local presence**, then K10 campaign pack fed by the manifest.
6. **K7 deploy for CARSI/RA**: biggest slice. Hosting is DigitalOcean App Platform; Synthex opens a PR on the client repo and DigitalOcean builds `main` after the human merge.

## Onboarding authority

Decided by the founder on 22/10/2026: Synthex creates GA4 properties, not just reads them, under authority the client grants at sign-up or onboarding. Onboarding must capture the client's Google consent for:

- `analytics.edit` (create and configure GA4 properties; today the app requests only `analytics.readonly` at `app/api/auth/oauth/[platform]/route.ts:130`),
- `webmasters` and `indexing` (Search Console, K1),
- `business.manage` (Business Profile, K5 and K6).

Each manifest records `onboardingAuthority.scopesRequired` and `onboardingAuthority.grants` (which scope, granted by whom, on what date). Grants stay `null` and listed in `unknown` until the client grants them.

## Cost per build

Founder rule, 22/10/2026: at most US$10–12 of direct spend (model calls, paid APIs) per client build. The manifest carries `costCeilingUsd` (default and maximum 12) and each kit carries `maxDirectCostUsd`, marked `estimate` until a real run measures it.

- Every kit run reports its actual spend.
- Kits fail closed: when the running total for the build would exceed the ceiling, the kit stops and reports; it never continues on a partial budget.
- Enforcement reuses the existing governor and budget enforcer (`lib/ai/budget-enforcer.ts`, `lib/ai/governor/`, `OrgBudgetPolicy` in `prisma/schema.prisma`), not a new mechanism.
- This is a spend-safety control only. No pricing, billing or payment work belongs to this spec.

## Founder gates

- Any paid SERP, keyword-volume or answer-engine provider (spend).
- A database table for kits or a suppression list (protected migrations).

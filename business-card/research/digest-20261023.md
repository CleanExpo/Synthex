# Living Card research digest: 23/10/2026

This digest condenses five research pages and one tactic register in the second-brain wiki (`~/2nd Brain/2nd Brain/Wiki/living-card/`). It is for building the card and the review-reply backend.

**Tags.** [VERIFIED] means the coordinating session reopened the source on 23/10/2026 and it says this. [VERIFIED-sub] means a research subagent fetched the source and it was not reopened. [INFERENCE] and [UNCONFIRMED] mean what they say. **None of this is legal advice.**

**Client metric moved.** Google reviews and replies help a business win more customers through conversion. Same-day consented follow-up turns event contacts into enquiries. Card sales add MRR for the card product.

## 2. Compliance flags on the card as it stands

1. **Spinning-wheel questions: RISK, fix before selling.**
   - Google allows asking for reviews only "without … attempting to influence the rating or the contents of the review". It also says merchants should not "request that specific content be included" [VERIFIED: 7400114].
   - Five of the six wheel questions lean positive.
   - Fix: neutral wording, labelled as optional "ideas if you're stuck", skippable, and never required.
2. **Prize draw ("you're in the draw"): breach if it is ever linked to a review.**
   - Google bans incentives for "any review" [VERIFIED: 7400114].
   - Keep it on a separate screen, with separate copy and timing.
3. **Event use on Phill's own phone: breach if a visitor types their review on it.**
   - Visitors must scan or tap through to their own phone [INFERENCE: conflict-of-interest and premises clauses, 7400114].
4. **Write-review link format: UNCONFIRMED.**
   - `search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=` is not documented on any Google page found.
   - Prefer the owner's "Get more reviews" link, or the Places API `googleMapsLinks.writeAReviewUri` [VERIFIED-sub: developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/maps-links].
5. **Video tip may over-promise.**
   - Business Profile videos are allowed: "Up to 30 seconds long", "Up to 75 MB", "720p or higher" [VERIFIED: support.google.com/business/answer/6103862].
   - Google's review help mentions only photos in the review form. Video is a separate "photo or video updates" contribution [VERIFIED: support.google.com/maps/answer/6230175, reopened 23/10/2026]. Change the tip to "add photos to your review". Invite video separately as a Maps update or a testimonial (improvement 13).
6. **Post-event emails need recorded consent.**
   - A handed-over card is not clear consent [VERIFIED: ACMA "Inferred consent is not as reliable…"; VERIFIED-sub: Spam Act s.4 2016 compilation].
7. **Video testimonials need consent records.**
   - Images and sound are personal information, and consent "may [be] withdraw[n] … at any time" [VERIFIED-sub: OAIC].
   - Testimonials must not be edited to mislead [VERIFIED: ACCC online-reviews page].
8. **Reply backend: possible Google OAuth blocker. UNCONFIRMED but material.**
   - Developers on Google's forum report that `business.manage` blocks all non-test users, with no way to apply for verification. Posts are dated 23/07/2026, 17/08/2026 and 21/10/2026 [VERIFIED-sub: discuss.google.dev thread 384175; the bug itself is unconfirmed].
   - Prove it with one real non-test client before selling the reply backend.

## 4. What is not proven, so don't sell it as fact

- That replying to reviews improves **ranking**. Google doesn't say so, and Whitespark experts rank it #122 for the map pack [VERIFIED].
- That video testimonials lift sales. The academic evidence is mixed [VERIFIED-sub: PMC8617416].
- The "21× / 100× / 391%" speed-to-lead figures. These are US web leads, from vendor studies. "391%" has no published method [VERIFIED-sub: lc-05].
- Any Australian consumer data. BrightLocal is US-only.

# Voice and lexicon — Greg-style writer (for Phill)

Counts are from the 16/10/2026 corpus: talk = 20 video transcripts (~125k words); write = 20 newsletter essays (~19k words). His spoken and written registers differ, so pick the register by format.

## Register by format

- **Spoken (video scripts, voice notes, podcast intros):** casual, excited, lots of "super", "cool", "incredible", "huge", "insane", and "the reality is" / "the way I think about it". Filler like "basically" (246 uses) and "literally" is natural on camera but should be trimmed to one or two per script.
- **Written (posts, newsletters, pages):** tighter; "boring" as a compliment (31 uses in 19k words), "niche", "cash", "empire"-style ambition words, short one-line paragraphs, the odd "mega" or "spicy".

Australianise: "mate" sparingly, "tradies", "reckon", "heaps" only if Phill uses them; spelling: organise, colour, programme (CARSI courses = "course"), metre, cheque.

## Greg's trademarks — do NOT use as Phill's own

These are his identity markers. Using them makes Phill sound like a copy:

- Sign-off "Be well," and "I'm rooting for you." Phill writes his own close.
- His coined names: tweet-sized landing pages (TSLPs), Santa Claus marketing, multipreneur/multipreneurship, the Two Coffee Rule, Threadmill effect, vibe marketing / vibe manufacturing as _his_ frameworks, "marry the niche, date the product", Community Empire, Late Checkout, Boring Marketing, Startup Ideas Podcast, idea browser.
- Fine to _reference with credit_ ("as Greg Isenberg puts it…") when the idea is genuinely his.

## AI-slop words and habits to ban

delve, tapestry, testament, landscape, realm, navigate (figurative), unlock, unleash, elevate, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, game-changer (overused), "in today's fast-paced world", "it's important to note", "let's dive in" at the start, em-dash chains, triplet adjectives, "not just X, but Y" on repeat, rhetorical "Imagine a world where…" openers, ending with "In conclusion".

Commodity sentences (true of everyone) get cut or replaced with Phill's specific version: not "fast response matters in water damage", but "the first job I lost was because we called back 40 minutes late" — **only if that story is real**.

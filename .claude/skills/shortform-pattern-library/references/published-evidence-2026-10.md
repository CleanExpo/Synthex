# Short-form vertical video: evidence-based viral pattern library

Compiled 19 October 2026 from web research (WebSearch + WebFetch). Audience: a marketing agency serving Australian restoration, cleaning, construction and training businesses.

## 0. Read this first: what the evidence can and cannot tell you

- **Most quantified data describes median brand performance, not 1M+ virality.** Socialinsider, Buffer, Emplifi and Metricool data measures typical business accounts. Median Reel views sit around 4,700–10,000. A finding like "45–60s Reels get the best engagement rate" means "best for the median brand post". It does not mean "45–60s is what goes viral".
- **Only two datasets found split out a viral tier, and both come from vendors:**
  - OpusClip scraped 1,449 TikToks. Its viral tier is the top 10%, 5.1M+ views, with a median length of 41s.
  - The Content Labs analysed 14,424 TikTok and Instagram videos and measured each video's views against its own account's typical views.
- **The strongest single predictor of virality in peer-reviewed work is account size.** The arXiv 2111.02452 study (400 labelled TikToks, 2021) found follower count was the strongest predictor. Content features came second: close-up and medium shot scale, on-screen text, point of view and video lifespan. Small accounts should expect to need many attempts per 1M+ hit.
- **Platform algorithms reward rates, not formats.** Instagram (Mosseri, 22 Jan 2025) named watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the top ranking signals. YouTube Shorts distribution rests on viewed vs swiped-away and on watch time. Every pattern below is a tactic for moving one of those rates.

Confidence tiers used throughout:
- **P (platform-official):** the platform's own help, blog or exec statement, at first or close second hand.
- **L (large-sample third party):** a study of thousands to millions of posts with some stated method.
- **V (vendor or opinion):** a tool vendor's blog, practitioner consensus or anecdote. Directional only.

### Hook archetypes: what the evidence says

| Archetype | Evidence | Notes for trades and restoration |
|---|---|---|
| **Question hook** (spoken) | Averaged **10.08x** the account's typical views vs **7.04x** for statement hooks (+43%). The share of videos doubling the account baseline was nearly identical (30.4% vs 29.9%), so questions raise the **ceiling** rather than the hit rate. 14,424 videos, 355 accounts, published 20 Aug 2026. https://thecontentlabs.app/blog/question-hooks-data-study | L/V (vendor, averages only, outlier-sensitive) | "Would you rip this carpet up or save it?" "What's the black stuff behind this skirting?" |
| **Curiosity gap / urgency** | One blog cites a "46,605 TikTok hooks" analysis in which curiosity and urgency beat direct-address and instructional openers. Original not found. https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-hook-formulas | V, unverified | "This is why your insurer will reject the claim..." |
| **Result-first / visual payoff in frame 1** | MrBeast memo: the first minute decides retention, and a crane lifting a house at ~30 s is an example of "wow factor". 36-page memo leaked Sept 2024. https://www.alexanderjarvis.com/memo-how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/ | V (long-form, but the principle transfers) | Show the "after" or the worst damage first, then rewind |
| **Close-up / medium shot as the opener** | Close-up and medium shot scales were among the top content features for virality (arXiv 2111.02452, 400 TikToks) https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.02452 | L (academic, small sample) | Open tight on the stain, mould or crack, not on a wide room |
| **Text hook on screen** | Presence of text was a virality feature in arXiv 2111.02452. Socialinsider (2023, 600K+ TikToks) found **TikTok captions under 10 words** had a higher view rate https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/how-long-are-tiktok-videos | L | Keep the on-screen hook to about 4–8 words, readable at 5–10 words per second |
| **Extreme / contrarian wording** | MrBeast memo: "I Survived" beats "I Spent". "Bananas Are The Worst Food On Earth" beats "I Don't Like Bananas" | V (title/CTR principle) | "Never use bleach on mould" beats "Bleach isn't ideal for mould" |
| **"Did you know", list ("3 things"), POV, pattern interrupt** | **No quantified comparison found** across ~6 hook-related searches. Practitioner consensus only. | V | See Contested |

**Practical hook rule set (synthesis, not measured):**
- The first frame must already show the subject at close or medium scale.
- Text hook of 4–8 words, on screen by about 0.5 s.
- Spoken hook finished by about 3 s. A question form is worth testing.
- The payoff is promised explicitly ("by the end you'll know...") or shown, as a before/after flash.

**Synthesis by goal:**
- **Reach to non-followers (IG/TT/YT):** 20–45 s, and under 60 s.
- **Watch time / authority (TikTok, FB, LinkedIn):** 60–180 s, provided retention holds.
- **Loops / replays:** 7–15 s. A secondhand claim (Klap, 500 viral Reels, Oct 2024–Mar 2025) puts the best completion and shareability at 7–15 s; the source is V and was not opened.

### Pacing and structure

| Parameter | Value | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cut / visual change frequency | One cut or visual change every **2–4 s** | OpusClip blog ("data suggests", unsourced); InfluenceFlow (rule of thumb) | V |
| Average clip length in top Shorts | 2.5 s average clip, correlated with "35% higher completion" (top 100 Shorts, 2024) | vidpros https://vidpros.com/video-clip-length/ | V, unverified |
| New story beat (story/BTS) | Every 5–7 s | OpusClip blog | V |
| Speed-ups for action footage | 1.2–1.5x | OpusClip blog | V |
| Script compression | Cut the first draft by about 30% | OpusClip blog | V |
| Re-engagement | MrBeast: a "3-minute re-engagement" plus another around minute 6. "No dull moments." "Video endings must always be abrupt to protect retention." | MrBeast memo (Sept 2024) | V (long-form). For Shorts, scale to roughly a re-hook every 3–5 s and an abrupt end. |
| Payoff placement | Swipes concentrate in the last 20% (42%, Dashlet), so **deliver the payoff late, then end immediately**. Do not signal "that's it" early. | arXiv 2204.12954 plus MrBeast memo | L plus V |
| Editing-pace academic study | University of Tartu 2026 thesis "The impact of video duration and editing pace on attention and engagement in TikTok" https://dspace.ut.ee/items/cb8dc46c-0841-4fed-91af-17db99f1b2d4/full | **UNAVAILABLE: fetch failed (ECONNREFUSED)**. Findings unknown, not absent. |

**Retention curve shapes (practitioner model, V):**
- A healthy Short shows a steep 0–3 s drop, then a flat plateau, then a small end spike.
- Above 100% average viewed indicates looping. Loops now also inflate Shorts "views" (since Mar 2025), but not "engaged views".

**Trades translation:**
- Open on the damage itself, close-up and handheld POV: the moisture meter reading, the mould bloom, the crack.
- Cut to the expert's face for the "why".
- End on the transformation.

**What transfers:**
- Hook in the first 3 s.
- Captions burned in.
- 9:16 with central safe zone.
- Close-up opening.
- Before/after payoff.
- Original footage with no watermark. Instagram down-ranks other platforms' watermarks, so **export clean masters for each platform**.

**What does not transfer:**
- Trending-audio dependence: business-account licensing restrictions apply, and LinkedIn and FB audiences are less trend-literate.

## 9. Recommended production spec (synthesis for the agency; each line traces to the tables above)

1. **Length:** produce one 30–45 s master. Cut a 12–20 s loop version for Shorts and Reels. Make a 60–120 s extended explainer for FB, LinkedIn and TikTok.
2. **0–0.5 s:** close-up of the problem (damage, stain, mould, crack) plus a 4–8-word text hook in the upper-middle third.
3. **0.5–3 s:** spoken hook, preferably a question or contrarian claim, promising a payoff.
4. **Body:** a visual change every 2–4 s. Burned-in captions, Montserrat Black or TikTok Sans Bold, white with black stroke, keyword highlighted. 1–3-word chunks for punchy content, 5–7 for explainers. Keep inside the safe zone (≥480–670 px clear at the bottom, ≥120 px on the right).
5. **Payoff:** the before/after reveal late in the video, then an **abrupt end**. Optionally loop back to frame 1.
6. **Audio:** voice-led, captioned for sound-off, licensed music bed, mastered to -14 LUFS / -1 dBTP.
7. **Post:**
   - Caption carries the search keyword phrase (e.g. "water damage restoration Brisbane").
   - 3–5 specific hashtags.
   - 3–5+ posts per week.
   - New formats go out as Trial Reels first.
   - Judge on viewed % and sends per reach, not likes.

## 10. Contested / weak evidence

- **Socialinsider sample-size contradiction.** The Instagram length numbers are identical on two Socialinsider pages, but one states **6M Reels** (Jan–Jun 2026) and the other **111K videos across 4 platforms**. Treat the IG numbers as directional, and treat the sample size as unresolved.
- **Ideal length is metric-dependent and studies conflict.**
  - Socialinsider puts the IG peak at 45–60 s and the TikTok views peak at 120–180 s.
  - Buffer finds >60 s gets more TikTok reach.
  - OpusClip's viral tier has a median of 41 s, with 15–30 s over-represented.
  - Fanpage Karma finds 0–10 s gets the most reach per follower.
  - A 315-reel study (via Klap) puts engagement per view highest at 15–30 s.
  - Socialinsider's 2023 TikTok study found view rate fell 16.55% beyond 15 s.
  - Results shift year to year. Length is "unlikely to be make-or-break" (Buffer's own data scientist). Hook and content quality dominate.
- **Median-brand data ≠ viral data.** See Section 0. Only vendor data (OpusClip, Content Labs) isolates top performers.
- **Mosseri signals.** The three-signal statement is confirmed via Social Media Today (22 Jan 2025). The widely repeated "sends weigh 3–5x likes" figure is **not** a Mosseri statement and is unverified.
- **Hook-type superiority.** The question-hook edge (+43%) comes from one vendor and averages only (no medians or significance). The 46,605-hook curiosity study could not be traced to a primary source. No measured data found for "did you know", POV, list or pattern-interrupt hooks.
- **Swipe-timing thresholds.** "1.5 s", "50–60% of drop-off in the first 3 s" and "70% viewed" targets are all vendor or unsourced. YouTube publishes no benchmark.
- **Sound-off statistics are old.** Fors Marsh 1.7 s / 0.25 s (2016), FB 85% (undated, circa 2016), Verizon/Publicis 69% (2019), FB captions +12% (2016), Kantar 88% sound-essential (2021, TikTok-commissioned). **No 2024+ replacement found.**
- **Trending audio.** The "2.5x views" Hootsuite claim has no primary source. The trending-audio benefit for business accounts is unproven, and licensing limits apply.
- **LUFS targets.** No official platform specification found. Figures from -10 to -14 LUFS are vendor guidance.
- **Safe-zone pixels.** Guides disagree (IG bottom clearance 480–670 px). Verify against current Meta and TikTok ad-spec pages and an on-device preview.
- **Caption style retention claims** (Hormozi/Submagic "15–25% higher retention"). Vendor-only. No independent A/B test found.
- **Cuts per second / average shot length.** No measured study retrieved. The Tartu 2026 thesis was **unavailable (connection refused)**, so its findings are unknown.
- **LinkedIn reach decline (-36% personal-profile median video reach).** Single third-party source.
- **TikTok "1 in 4 search within 30 s".** Attributed to TikTok newsroom; primary not retrieved.
- **No quantified source found (stated scope: 1–3 searches each):** loop endings and open loops; zoom punch-ins; colour-grading trends; SFX density; AI-voiceover prevalence or penalty; emoji usage; Instagram Classic/Modern font names; "The Bold Font" / Komika Axis; font size relative to frame; collab lift; Shorts posting cadence. Practitioner consensus exists for each (e.g. punch-in every few seconds, whoosh on cuts, loop the last line into the first) but is **opinion only**.
- **Niche 1M+ examples.** Account-level figures come from press and promotional sources at mixed dates (2021–2025). Specific reel URLs with verified view counts were not retrieved. No professional restoration or mould creator at 1M+ was found in the stated search scope.
